# 로어북 교체용 — [Veridia — Land, Manor, and Town]

> 2026-08-17 로어북 다이어트. 리수 로어북의 해당 항목을 아래 블록으로 **통째로 교체**한다.
>
> **왜**: 옛 판에는 각 방향에 뭐가 사는지("Living there: goblin tribes…")와 수맥 위치
> ("clear water sits behind bedrock…")가 적혀 있었다. 로어북은 매 턴 실리므로 모델이
> 0턴부터 정답을 안다 — 실사고: 아데레가 첫 장면에서 "마력 감응으로 수맥이 느껴진다"며
> 탐사도 없이 물 문제의 답을 불어 버림.
>
> **어디로 갔나**: estate-vars.js의 `CANON` 표 (정본 발견 6개 — 고블린 find_n_2 ·
> 수맥/옛 수로 find_n_3 · 하피 find_n_5 · 거대 거미 find_w_4 · 오크 find_s_5 ·
> 리자드맨 find_e_4). 해당 자리의 find 이벤트가 터지는 턴에만 통지로 실린다.
> 남는 로어북은 "집무실 낡은 지도" 수준 — 어디가 어떤 지형인지까지만.

```
### 🏛️ [Veridia — Land, Manor, and Town]

### [1. The Land]
* Name: Barony of Veridia, called The Abandoned Shield.
* Scale: County-sized ground held under a baron's title.
* Shape: A natural fortress and a natural prison — mountains north,
  forest west, plains south, river east. One way in from the south road.
* Governance: There is no bureaucracy here. Nothing happens that the
  Baron does not personally set in motion.
* Survey: This ground has never been surveyed. What each direction actually
  holds is not settled until scouts bring it back — do not decide it in advance.
  Nobody in the barony, whatever their talents, knows more than the old map
  in the study shows: terrain and nothing else.

### [2. The Four Directions]
Terrain is fixed and visible from the keep. What lies inside it is not.

#### 북 산맥 — The Razor Ridge (칼날 능선)
* Steep bare rock. The route monsters use to come down.
* Visible from below: loose stone, scree, snow that does not leave the peaks.

#### 서 숲 — The Whispering Woods (속삭이는 숲)
* Old growth, close-packed. Sunlight barely reaches the floor.
* Visible from the edge: timber, game, wild herbs at the treeline.

#### 남 평원 — The Ashen Plains (잿빛 평원)
* Flat to the horizon, under a grey ash crust gone hard as clay.
* Visible: open ground. Whether anything will grow in it is another question.

#### 동 강가 — The Riverbank (강가)
* Fast, silt-heavy water running east to the coast.
* Visible: a waterway, and no dock to use it with.

### [3. The Centre — Village and Keep]
* Ruined curtain wall, collapsed cottages, a well gone foul.
* Water: the standing problem. Rain must be boiled or water carried up
  from the river.

### [4. The Manor — Fading Hills]
Three storeys of stone. Structurally sound, stripped bare. A monument to
somebody else's excess.
* 1F: vaulted grand hall with the great staircase, ballroom (parquet floor,
  silver chandeliers), industrial kitchen with cold hearths.
* 2F: guest wing of six suites, stripped to the walls. Lord's study — the
  mahogany desk is too heavy to have been carried off, and the barony map
  is fixed to the wall.
* 3F: drawing rooms and storage, thick with dust.
* Cellar: an extensive dry network. Would hold a great deal, or a cellar's
  worth of wine.
* Grounds: a formal garden run wild over a dry marble fountain, rare
  plantings choked out. Stone barracks (forty men at capacity) and a
  training yard with rotted posts.

### [5. The Ruined Chapel]
* On a low hill above the village. Roof fallen in, altar defiled.
* It was a branch house of the Sisterhood of Luminous Grace.
* If it is rebuilt and a donation reaches the High Church, the Sisterhood
  dispatches a Luminary. She arrives as a person, is named, and stays —
  healing and care become possible for the first time.

### [6. Zoning — Wind and Water]
* Prevailing wind: northwest to southeast, mountain toward river.
* Water: west to east, spring toward river.
* The rule: clean things upwind and upstream, dirty things downwind and
  downstream. Academy-trained maids are taught this and they notice when
  it is broken.

* [High Keep — centre] The central hill on bedrock, above any flood and
  hard to approach unseen. Administration, defence, treasury.
* [Sunset Terrace — west, upwind] Gentle slope by the mountain spring.
  Quarters, bathhouse on direct spring water, temple and clinic.
  The pleasant air and the clean water are both here.
* [Smoke Valley — east, downwind] Rocky low ground by the river mouth.
  Forge, tannery, kiln, drainage. The wind takes the smoke away from
  the town and out over the water.
* [Fertile Flats — south] Soft open soil running into the plains.
  Stables, granaries, windmills — and the animal smell kept off the houses.
* [River Landing — east bank] Mud banks along the water. Market, warehouses,
  guild halls, taverns. Where foreign merchants would come ashore, if there
  were anything to come ashore for.
```

## 삭제된 원문 (참조용 — 다시 넣지 말 것)

- §2 각 방향의 `Living there:` 4줄 (goblin tribes+harpies / giant spiders+plants /
  orc raiders / lizardmen) → CANON find_n_2·n_5·w_4·s_5·e_4
- §3 `Known but unreached:` 문단 (rear hills bedrock 수맥 + aqueduct 재건 —
  "everyone local knows it is there") → CANON find_n_3. **"모두가 안다" 설정 자체를 폐기**하고
  "아무도 지도 이상은 모른다"(§1 Survey 추가 문장)로 뒤집었다.
