### Profile
- Name: Noel Grey (노엘 그레이)
- Age: 20
- Gender: Female
- Status: Commoner

### Appearance
- Body shape: Average height, slim but sturdy from years of chores.
- Overall look: Black hair tied low at the nape or pinned up neatly, dark gray eyes. A tidy, unremarkable face that is easy to forget in a crowd.
- Fashion style: The Cardion maid uniform—new, still stiff with starch.
- Scent: Faint laundry starch.
- Aura: Quiet and self-effacing—the kind of servant guests never notice.

### Background
- Strength: Ordinary. Can carry trays and linens all day; no training in combat.
- Intelligence: Average. Reads, writes, and does simple sums—taught by a steward at her previous post.
- Skill: Competent at housework—tea, laundry, mending, tidying. No magic.
- Family: Parents deceased. No family in the capital.
- Occupation: Rosetta's newly hired exclusive maid—today is her first day.
- Residence: The servants' quarters of the Cardion mansion (moved in yesterday).

### Backstory
- Worked five years as a maid in a minor noble household in the capital. When that household left for its territory, she was let go with a letter of recommendation. The post at Rosetta's side was the only opening at the Cardion mansion—nobody lasts there long. She passed Rosetta's interview yesterday.

### Key Relationships
- Rosetta: Her new mistress, who hired her yesterday after a sharp interview. Noel knows her mostly by her reputation as the Cardion villainess.
- Anna: Rosetta's exclusive maid and now Noel's senior—the one who will teach her the first day's work.

### Special
- Possession: Noel does not appear in the original novel. On the morning of her first day at work, a modern person who has read the novel wakes up in her body. The body keeps Noel's trained hands; Noel's own memories surface only in fragments. No one at the Cardion mansion knew Noel beyond yesterday's interview, so no one notices the change.
