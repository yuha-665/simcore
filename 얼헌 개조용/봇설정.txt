- The System (Status Window{{#if_pure {{equal::{{getvar::lore}}::1}}}}, Alter Store{{/if}}, Skills{{#if_pure {{equal::{{getvar::stats}}::1}}}}, Stats{{/if}}) is intrinsic to each Awakened individual. - World Name: Earth (Post-Gate Emergence), aka Alter-Earth.
- Setting: Modern Earth, decades post-'Gate Emergence'. Cities adapted with Hunter infrastructure (Guild Halls, Hunter Districts,{{#if_pure {{equal::{{getvar::lore}}::1}}}} Alter Store access,{{/if}} monster byproduct industries). Gates appear globally, leading to 'Dungeons' (separate dimensions). Constant low-level threat of 'Gate Breaks'. Tech coexists with supernatural; conventional weapons fail inside dungeons, but tech supports Hunters outside.
- Society: Stratified; Awakened Hunters are an influential class. Guilds manage Hunters, contracts, Gate access, wield political/economic power.{{#if_pure {{equal::{{getvar::scenario}}::0}}}} Multinational corporations invest heavily in Gates (R&D, gear, resources{{#if_pure {{equal::{{getvar::lore}}::1}}}}, Alter Store{{/if}}). Government agencies attempt oversight, often clashing with Guilds/Corps. Public views Hunters with mix of awe, fear, resentment, dependence. High risk/reward system; mobility requires "blood and sweat."{{#if_pure {{equal::{{getvar::lore}}::1}}}} Alter Store is a mundane necessity.{{/if}}{{/if}}
- Law: Pre-Gate laws exist alongside new Gate-specific legislation (licensing, containment, ability use, resource taxation). Specialized Hunter Courts exist. Gate/resource ownership contested. Nonetheless, thriving black market for illicit goods/services.
